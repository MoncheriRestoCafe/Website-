A Note from the Founder

On the Art
of Lunch

Sanatha · Founder, The Lunch Club

Lunch should not be an afterthought. It should not be a rushed transaction between two meetings, eaten standing at a counter, washed down with cold coffee. It should be something you look forward to — a pause in the day that reminds you why good work and good food belong together.

When I started The Lunch Club, I was not trying to solve a logistics problem. I was trying to restore a ritual. I had spent years watching talented, driven people eat badly — not because they did not care, but because the city had not offered them anything worth caring about.

So we built something different. A kitchen where cooks take pride in the grain of their rice. Where a bowl of dal is seasoned the way your grandmother would have — patiently, intuitively, without shortcuts. Where the packaging is considered enough to be part of the experience.

The Lunch Club is not a subscription. It is a membership — and the distinction matters. A membership implies trust, continuity, and mutual respect. Welcome to the club. We are glad you are here.

Sanatha
Founder  ·  The Lunch Club

What We Stand For

Our
Promise

Three Pillars

Three principles underpin everything that leaves our kitchen. They are not policies — they are the standards we hold ourselves to, every single day.

"We do not just deliver food. We deliver a standard — and that standard travels every metre between our kitchen and your table."
04
01

Quality

We source from producers we trust and ingredients we can name. Every bowl is assembled to order, never ahead of time, never in bulk. The rice is cooked fresh. The greens are washed the morning they are used. We do not cut corners on things you cannot see — because those are precisely the things you taste.

02

Packaging

Your lunch arrives in packaging designed to preserve temperature, protect texture, and reduce waste. We use food-safe, compostable materials wherever possible. Our containers are built to travel — so that by the time the meal reaches your desk, it still feels like it was made for you. Because it was.

03

Reliability

Noon means noon. We know your schedule is not flexible, and we do not treat your time casually. Our delivery windows are tight, our tracking is transparent, and if anything falls short of what we promised, we make it right — no forms, no delays. Your trust is worth more than our efficiency.

The Menu

Three
Families.

One Kitchen

Our menu is not a list — it is a philosophy made edible. Everything we cook falls into one of three families, each designed for a different kind of appetite. All three share the same kitchen, the same standards, and the same uncompromising attention to how a meal ought to feel.

"We cook the way we wish someone would cook for us — as if time were not a constraint and flavour were the only currency that mattered."

— The Kitchen, The Lunch Club
06
I
For the Body

Power Bowls

Architecture for appetite. Our bowls are built — not assembled — around a protein, a grain, a fresh element, and a sauce that ties it all together. High in protein, precise in calories, and quietly satisfying in a way that most "healthy" food refuses to be.

II
For the Soul

Traditional Meals

Recipes that carry memory. Our traditional meals are drawn from the deep culinary grammar of South India — slow-cooked, fully seasoned, and served with everything they were always meant to come with. This is not nostalgia. It is craft.

III
For the Week

Daily Specials

A rotating calendar of comfort. Our chefs decide each week's rotation based on season, mood, and instinct. What arrives on Tuesday may never come again in quite the same form. That is precisely the point — a lunch worth anticipating.
